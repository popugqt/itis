### <span style="background-color: rgba(213, 142, 173, 0.30);">Первый замечательный предел</span>

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin{x}}{x}=1
$$

$$
S_{\triangle OAB}\le S_{\underset{сектор}{OAB}}\le S_{\triangle OAD}
$$

1. $$
S_{\triangle OAB}=\frac{OA\cdot OB}{2}
$$

$$
OA=OB=1
$$

$$
\sin{x}=\frac{AB}{OA}
$$

$$
AB=\sin{x}
$$

$$
S_{\triangle OAB}=\frac{\sin{x}}{2}
$$

2. $$
S_{\underset{сектор}{OAB}}=x\cdot\frac{OB^2}{2}
$$

$$
OB=1
$$

$$
S_{\underset{сектор}{OAB}}=\frac{x}{2}
$$

3. $$
S_{\triangle OAD}=\frac{OA\cdot AD}{2}
$$

$$
OA=1
$$

$$
\tan{x}=\frac{AD}{OA}
$$

$$
AD=\tan{x}
$$

$$
S_{\triangle OAD}=\frac{\tan{x}}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{\sin{x}}{2}\le\frac{x}{2}\le\frac{\tan{x}}{2}
$$

$$
\sin{x}\le x\le\frac{\sin{x}}{\cos{x}}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{\sin{x}}\ge\frac{1}{x}\ge\frac{\cos{x}}{\sin{x}}
$$

$$
\cos{x}\le\frac{\sin{x}}{x}\le1
$$

$$
\lim_{x\to 0}{\cos{x}}=1\quad\lim1=1
$$

---

**По теореме о двух милиционерах:**

$$
\lim_{x\to 0}{\frac{\sin{x}}{x}}=1
$$
